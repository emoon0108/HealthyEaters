# Healthy Eaters Mini-Games

All games are food-themed and designed for short, token-gated play sessions after a child completes a meal goal.

| Game | Cost | Time | Simple Goal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Food Match | 1 | 45s | Match foods to fruits, vegetables, dairy, grains, or protein. |
| Garden Catch | 1 | 45s | Catch falling fruits and vegetables while avoiding candy and soda. |
| Plate Builder | 2 | 60s | Drag foods onto a plate to build a balanced meal. |
| Texture Sort | 1 | 45s | Sort foods into crunchy, soft, smooth, and chewy groups. |
| Color Plate | 1 | 45s | Fill a plate with red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple foods. |
| Memory Meal | 1 | 60s | Flip cards to match healthy food pairs. |
| Food Maze | 2 | 60s | Move through a maze and collect meal ingredients. |
| Snack Tap | 1 | 30s | Tap healthy snacks and skip sugary snacks. |
| Recipe Order | 2 | 60s | Put healthy recipe steps in the correct order. |
| Nutrient Quest | 3 | 60s | Collect foods that give body powers like bone, eye, and energy power. |

## Design Notes

- Games should be calm, colorful, and low-pressure.
- Sessions should be short enough to feel like a reward without taking over mealtime.
- Games should reward effort and food curiosity, not perfection.
- Parents should eventually be able to enable, disable, or set limits for each game.
