# Healthy Eaters Points Shop

Children earn game tokens by completing food goals. They spend tokens to play mini-games. Finished mini-games award points. Points are used for digital rewards that make the app feel personal without adding pressure around food.

## Economy Loop

1. Complete food goal.
2. Earn game tokens.
3. Spend tokens to play food-themed mini-games.
4. Earn points from completed games.
5. Spend points on customization, collections, and celebrations.

## Shop Categories

| Category | What It Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Food Buddy | Hats, outfits, glasses, and visual buddy customization. |
| Plate Themes | Different plates, table colors, and food-friendly backgrounds. |
| Food Facts | Collectible fact cards about foods the child is learning. |
| Virtual Garden | Seeds that grow into fruits and vegetables over time. |
| Titles | Profile names like Taste Explorer or Rainbow Builder. |
| Celebrations | Completion animations such as confetti or calm stars. |

## Design Rule

Points should never be used to pressure eating. They should make the child feel proud, safe, and in control of their app world.
